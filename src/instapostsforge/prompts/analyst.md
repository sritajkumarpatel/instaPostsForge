## Category: pick exactly one

| Category | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| landscape | Mountains, coast, forest, fields, rivers: nature where the place is the subject |
| sky | Sunrise, sunset, clouds or sky colour as the main subject |
| city | Buildings, skylines, interiors, bridges: architecture or the built environment |
| street | Everyday public life, candid moments, markets, traffic |
| people | One or a few people as the clear subject: portraits, friends, groups |
| food | Dishes, drinks, ingredients, cafés where the food is the subject |
| animals | Pets, strays, birds, wildlife |
| macro | Close-ups where texture or a small subject fills the frame |
| night | After dark: stars, city lights, long exposures |
| travel | A moment that is clearly about being on a trip: transport, a landmark with the traveller's view, a journey |
| event | Festivals, weddings, concerts, celebrations, sports |
| abstract | Shapes, shadows, colour fields, patterns, minimal compositions |

If two fit, choose the one a viewer would name first. A sunset over the sea is "sky" if the sky fills most of the frame and "landscape" otherwise.

## Mood: pick exactly one

calm, joyful, energetic, nostalgic, dramatic, romantic, playful, awe. Choose the feeling the photo gives a viewer, not the feeling of the people in it.

## Quality score from 1 to 10

Score it as a scrolling viewer would: sharpness, exposure, composition, and how strongly it stops the scroll.
- 1 to 3: blurry, badly exposed, accidental, or a screenshot.
- 4 to 6: a decent phone photo.
- 7 to 8: clearly strong; good light and composition.
- 9 to 10: exceptional; keep these rare.

## Flags: answer each honestly

- has_people: any person is recognisable as a person.
- has_children: anyone who appears to be under 16 is visible.
- has_identifiable_strangers: a face other than the account owner's is in focus and identifiable. If unsure, say true.
- has_text_or_logo: readable text, a sign, or a brand logo is prominent.
- is_screenshot_or_meme: a screenshot, meme, document, receipt or mostly text.
- shows_private_home: the outside of a private home, a house number, a street address or a car number plate is readable. This only keeps the address out of the text; such photos are still posted.
- is_explicit: nudity, sexual content or graphic gore. Ordinary rooms, homes, cars and belongings are not explicit.

## Main subject

Name the one thing the photo is of in one or two lowercase words: moon, dog, sunset, dosa, temple, dragonfly. Use the plainest common word and the same word every time, so a later moon photo is also "moon", not "lunar glow" or "night sky". The planner uses this to avoid posting the same subject on nearby days.
