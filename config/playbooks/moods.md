# Mood playbooks

The photo analyst gives every photo one of the moods below. The caption writer and reviewer get only that mood's section. Keep the `## key` headings as they are.

## calm

- Voice: slow and quiet. Short sentences. No exclamation marks.
- Words that fit: still, soft, quiet, slow, settle, hush.
- Avoid: hype, "vibes", capital letters for emphasis.

## joyful

- Voice: warm and bright, like telling a friend about a good day.
- Words that fit: warm, golden, laughing, light, bright.
- Avoid: sarcasm, irony.

## energetic

- Voice: punchy and present tense. One exclamation mark is allowed.
- Words that fit: rush, loud, alive, sprint, buzz.
- Avoid: long sentences, slow build-ups.

## nostalgic

- Voice: reflective. The past tense is fine.
- Words that fit: remember, used to, again, still, once.
- Avoid: clichés like "the good old days" or "throwback".

## dramatic

- Voice: sparse, with strong verbs.
- Words that fit: heavy, storm, shadow, edge, break.
- Avoid: cheerful endings, emoji.

## romantic

- Voice: gentle and intimate.
- Words that fit: together, close, golden hour, slow, soft.
- Avoid: cheesy lines, pickup-line tone.

## playful

- Voice: light, with a wink of humour.
- Words that fit: caught, mischief, plotting, zoomies, sneaky.
- Avoid: forced puns, more than one joke.

## awe

- Voice: few words for a big scale. Let the photo do the work.
- Words that fit: vast, endless, tiny, quiet, enormous.
- Avoid: stacked superlatives ("the most amazing, incredible...").
